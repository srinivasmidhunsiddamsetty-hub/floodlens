## v0.42: Soft light boxes and grouped facts (4 Nov 2026)

From the owner's notes on v0.41. The surface and the selection colour were picked from options (the selection colours rendered on the real map); the owner kept the not-reached wording.

- **A new surface for the boxes, "Soft light":** white fading to a cool grey from the top, a hairline and a faint inner highlight (`.surface-soft`).
  - It replaces the pale blue gradient, which the owner found recognisably "vibe-coded".
  - It's used by the two sea-level figure boxes and by the place card's "Water reaches at" box.
- **Height, Footprint and Low spot are grouped in one Soft light box** on the place card, instead of sitting straight on the card.
- **The selected building is vermilion again** (`#e8452c`, as in v0.20–v0.37). The owner found charcoal dull. Against the Plasma levels it measures at least ΔE 10 (9 for deuteranopia). The card's place tile follows it.
- **"Entire Miami Beach" shows a folded map.**
- **The left panel is a little wider:** 300 px (was 280).
- **Kept:** the not-reached answer "Up to +5 ft · Doesn't reach it" (options were offered).
- The screenshot clicks moved 10 px right with the wider panel: (632, 470) and (878, 346).
- Tests: 107 unit/component tests and 19 end-to-end tests.

## v0.41: Plasma, "By level" and better figure boxes (4 Nov 2026)

From the owner's notes on v0.40. The palette, the name and the box style were picked from options; the palettes were rendered on the real map first.

- **By level colours are now Plasma**, a standard science palette: yellow → orange → coral → magenta → purple → indigo (`#f0f921` `#fca636` `#e16462` `#b12a90` `#6a00a8` `#0d0887`).
  - The owner wanted a different colour for each level, easy to tell apart on the map without looking improvised. Shades of one orange didn't do it.
  - Neighbouring levels are now far apart: closest pair ΔE 15.1 for normal vision, against 3.9 before.
  - +4 ft's purple is close to the low-spot hatch colour; the hatch pattern keeps low spots apart. The charcoal selected building still stands out.
- **"By level" replaces "Individual"** (the owner's pick from "By level", "Added", "Per foot", "Breakdown").
- **The level chips are pills.**
- **The figure boxes match the place card's "Water reaches at" box:** a faint blue gradient with a hairline, replacing the flat grey-blue fill the owner found ordinary.
  - There's more room inside each box: 8 px from label to number, 10 px from number to switch.
  - The two boxes are the same height and 8 px apart. The slider grew from 168 to 224 px to make room.
- **Height, Footprint and Low spot values are semibold.**
- **Short windows:** the left panel now tightens below 750 px (was 700), so it still fits without scrolling down to about 650 px, with or without the low-spot line.
- Tests: 107 unit/component tests and 19 end-to-end tests. Screenshot `02-by-level-5ft` replaces `02-individual-5ft`.

## v0.40: The owner's swatches, "0 ft" and a frame icon (4 Nov 2026)

From the owner's notes on v0.39. The name for today, the chip corners and the icon were picked from options; the owner also asked whether the compass is needed and kept it.

- **Individual colours are now the owner's own swatches**, yellow → orange: `#f3f351` `#f0dd4a` `#f0b33f` `#ec9a38` `#e77631` `#e7612a` (0 ft to +5 ft). These are six of the eight colours in their reference, picked to stay furthest apart.
  - Measured, neighbouring levels are closer than v0.38's ramp: ΔE 3.9 for normal vision and 2.8 for colour-blind vision, against 7.3 and 6.8. The thin white edges between levels help tell them apart.
- **Today's level is "0 ft"**, everywhere: the slider, the legend, the place card's track, and its answer ("Water reaches at · 0 ft"). Every level now reads as a number.
- **The level chips have 6 px corners**, like the toggles and the figure boxes. They were square in v0.39.
- **"Entire Miami Beach" shows frame corners**, the usual "fit to view" symbol, instead of a house, which the owner found misleading.
- **The compass stays.** On a map you can turn, it's the standard way to show north. FloodLens's 3D view starts turned about 15°, so without it nothing says the map isn't north-up.
- **Suggested, not changed:** a primary colour other than blue (ink `#1f2937` recommended; deep teal `#0f766e` or deep navy `#1e3a5f` as alternatives). Blue stays until the owner picks one.
- Tests: 107 unit/component tests and 19 end-to-end tests (new: "0 ft" on the slider, the legend and the card; the frame icon; the new colours on the slider track).

## v0.39: Figures in boxes (4 Nov 2026)

From the owner's notes on v0.38: the figures looked like "text thrown on to the white box". The box style was picked from three options.

- Tests: 107 unit/component tests (new: the boxes, the unit switch; back: the unit setting) and 19 end-to-end tests (back: the unit switch, remembered after a reload).

## v0.36: Land underwater beside the slider (4 Nov 2026)

The owner sent a spec ("UI Refinements: Sea Level Rise Panel, Map Controls, and Right-Side Insights"). The layout of the figures (beside the slider, where "+2 ft" was) and keeping the low-spot area (under its switch) were picked from options.

- **The big "+2 ft" is gone from the Sea level card.** The slider's bold level name already says it. Its place holds the land underwater at that level, with the km² / acres switch under it.
- **The figures follow the Single / Layered toggle:**
  - **Single: "Total submerged area"**, everything underwater up to the level, beyond today's high tide, and its share of the city's land. At +3 ft: 7.43 km², 37%.
  - **Layered: "Additional submerged area"**, only what the level adds to the one below it. At +3 ft: 6.44 km², 32%.
  - The added figure is the difference between the two neighbouring totals, from the same NOAA numbers, so the added figures always sum to the total and nothing is counted twice. Both shares use the same denominator: the city's Census land area (19.9 km²).
  - At today's high tide both say "None yet".
- **Insights is only about what you pick on the map.** The "Land underwater" card is gone: its two figures moved left, and its chart and "+2 ft adds" row went with it. Hovering the chart used to spotlight a level on the map; that went too. With nothing picked, Insights shows the existing "Click any building" prompt.
- **The low-spot area moved under the Low spots switch**, while that layer is on: "232 acres at +2 ft".
- **The left panel still fits without scrolling** in windows down to about 650 px tall, even with the low-spot line. On short windows the slider gives up up to 16 px more height for that line (down to 100 px).
- **2D over 3D again**, stacked in the control column as in v0.34.
- **Zoom no longer covers the scale bar.** The zoom-out button overlapped the top of the scale bar by 7 px. The column now starts 88 px up, 12 px clear of it.
- Tests: 109 unit/component tests in 14 files (new: the figures in both modes and units, the cumulative/added sums, the low-spot line, Insights without city-wide figures; removed: the chart and "adds" tests) and 19 end-to-end tests (new: Single total vs Layered added at +1, +2 and +3 ft; the panel fits with the low-spot line on; 2D over 3D and zoom clear of the scale bar). ## v0.35: Owner's pass on v0.34 (3 Nov 2026)

The owner reviewed v0.34 and asked for these changes. The names, the control layout and removing the (i) were picked from side-by-side options.

- **The play button is gone** ("Step up to +5 ft").
- **The (i) next to the sea level is gone.** The caption "above today's high tide" already says what the number means, and "About the data" explains the levels.
- **The colour toggle sits beside the "Sea level" title**, and is named **Single / Layered** (was "Colours: Simple / Detailed"). The "Colours" label and the two captions under it ("One colour for all the water at this level", "A colour for each foot the sea rises") are gone.
- **The left panel fits without scrolling.** With the play button, the captions and the toggle row gone it needs 700 px of window height; the slider also gives up height on shorter windows (168 px down to 116 px), so it fits in windows down to about 650 px tall, the browser window of a 1366 × 768 laptop.
- **The legend shows only with Layered colours.** With one colour there's nothing to decode. It no longer has a "Low spot" row.
- **The legend takes the pointer.** Before, the map's hand cursor and hover labels showed through it.
- **New Layered colours, chosen by the owner:** +1 ft `#a6d8ed`, +2 ft `#6db6db`, +3 ft `#338ec4`, +4 ft `#1766a1`, +5 ft `#083b75`. Today is a lighter tint of the same blue, `#dceff5`.
  - Measured at 85% over the land, the closest neighbouring pair is OKLab ΔE 10.0 for normal vision and 9.1 for colour-blind vision (v0.34's ramp: 10.1 and 9.3).
  - +1 ft is almost the basemap's water colour (ΔE 1), and today is close to the land (ΔE about 3). Both bands are small: today covers no land in the city, and +1 ft adds 0.038 km².
- **Map controls in one slim column at the bottom right:** 2D/3D, "Entire Miami Beach", the compass, zoom. The top right of the map is clear.
- **A dial for turning and tilting.** In 3D the compass becomes a round dial: arrows above and below the needle tilt the view, arrows either side turn it, and clicking the needle faces north. It replaces the labelled "Rotate" and "Tilt" panel. The tooltips open just left of the column, never over another button.
- Tests: 107 unit/component tests (the play and (i) tests are replaced by one that checks neither is there; the legend tests cover Layered only and the pointer) and 19 end-to-end tests (the play test is replaced by one that checks the left panel fits at 900 and 650 px tall, with the toggle in the title row).

## v0.34: Review feedback (3 Nov 2026)

A product manager and a couple of users reviewed v0.33 and sent 14 points ("FloodLens v0.33 feedback"). This version acts on the ones that were settled. "Land underwater" stays where it is: its new place hasn't been decided.

- **Two colour modes** (#9), a "Colours" switch in the Sea level card, remembered in the browser:
  - **Simple** (the new default): all the water at the chosen level in one blue (`#3d84e6`), spreading as the sea rises.
  - **Detailed**: the area each foot adds in its own colour, as before.
  - The map, the slider track, the chart, the "adds" row, the Flooding swatch and the legend all follow the mode.
- **Clearer Detailed colours** (#1). People couldn't tell the v0.17 blues apart. The new ramp turns from sky blue through royal blue to indigo, so neighbouring levels differ in hue as well as lightness: `#80cffd` `#4ba3f7` `#3672e9` `#3a45c8` `#31248a` `#1c0b4d`.
  - Measured at 85% over the land, the worst neighbouring pair went from OKLab ΔE 8.8 to 10.1 for normal vision, and from 8.6 to 9.3 for colour-blind vision.
  - That is about the limit for six steps in the hues the basemap leaves free: green is parks, aqua is water, purple is low spots and red is the selection. A search of about a million candidate ramps found nothing clearly better.
- **The sea-level slider stands upright** (#2): today at the bottom and +5 ft at the top, so raising it reads as the sea rising. The big "+2 ft" sits beside it.
- **"Watch the water rise" is now "Step up to +5 ft"** (#7). Users read the old label as tides that rise and fall back.
- **Map controls** (#4, #6, #8):
  - Zoom moves to the bottom right, with the compass above it.
  - In 3D, a "Rotate" and "Tilt" panel sits above them, with buttons for the moves some trackpads and mice can't make: rotate 30° left or right, tilt 10° up or down.
  - 2D/3D and a new "Entire Miami Beach" button stay at the top right. The button uses a house icon, the usual map symbol for the default view (v0.4 had swapped a house for the frame icon).
  - Every map button shows its name in a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus.
- **A slim vertical colour scale replaces the legend box** (#5): only the sea-level colours, today at the bottom, the chosen level in bold. In Simple mode, the levels above the chosen one are empty outlines. Low spot joins it when that layer is on. The water, building, selected and city-limits keys are gone; the switches in "On the map" already show those swatches.
- **No panel behind the Insights cards** (#10): the two cards float over the map, which now runs under them, as in v0.27–v0.31. The camera is padded by the cards' width, so the city view, the area chips and "the middle of the map" use the part the cards don't cover. The left panel stays docked.
- **Separate building and spot cards** (#13): a spot or open water no longer shows empty Height and Footprint rows ("—"); it lists only Low spot. A building keeps all three.
- **New icons** (#12): a plot of land for Footprint (was a scan frame) and an arrow down to a line for Low spot (was a mountain, which suggested high ground).
- **Not done yet:** where "Land underwater" goes (#3, #11), and the wider typography and hierarchy pass (#12, #14). Both need design decisions first.
- Tests: 108 unit/component tests (new: the vertical scale in both modes, the spot card, the upright slider, the colour mode) and 19 end-to-end tests (new: the bottom controls with rotate and tilt; the colour modes, remembered after a reload).

## v0.33: Desktop and laptop only (1 Nov 2026)

- **FloodLens is now made for desktop and laptop screens only.** The phone and tablet layout is gone:
  - the drawer for the left panel and the button in the top bar that opened it;
  - the Insights bottom sheet and its summary strip;
  - the smaller chip and top-bar sizes for narrow screens, and the legend starting closed;
  - the extra room the opening view and the map credit left for the bottom sheet.
- **One layout at every size.** The page is made for screens 1024 px wide and up. A narrower window scrolls sideways instead of squeezing the map.
- Nothing changes on a laptop or desktop screen: the panels, map, chips and cards look as they did in v0.32.
- The docs no longer describe a phone layout. Older versions keep their phone screenshots as a record of what they were.
- Tests: 105 unit/component tests and 17 end-to-end tests (the phone-layout test is removed). Screenshots `05`–`07` (phone views) are no longer made.

## v0.32: Insights back in a panel (30 Oct 2026)

## v0.3: Clarity pass (27 Oct 2026)

Driven by a design review of v0.2. Each change lists the problem it solved.

**Map and data visualization**
- **Flood bands by water level.** v0.2 drew every scenario in the same blue, so you couldn't tell what flooded at +1 ft from what flooded at +3 ft. Each area is now coloured by the level at which NOAA's mapped extent *first* reaches it: navy for today, then blue, sky blue and aqua for +1/+2/+3 ft. Darker means reached sooner, so most exposed, which matches how depth maps read (the core floods first, the edges last). The bands are the validated neighbour differences (0→1, 1→2, 2→3 ft) that the pipeline already produced.
- **Comparison mode removed.** The "Compare two scenarios" toggle confused users. The bands show every level's contribution on one map, so the separate mode was redundant.
- **Spotlight.** Hovering a level in the legend, the chart or the breakdown list dims the other bands on the map.
- **Buildings in warm taupe grey** instead of near-white, for clear contrast against the pale-blue water and the blue bands. Selection is orange, which no data layer uses.
- **Building picking fixed:**
  - In 3D, a click now follows the view ray to the first building it enters. Before, a roof click hit the ground behind the building, and some buildings felt unclickable.
  - Building pieces split by map-tile edges are re-joined, which fixes the partial highlight.
  - The highlight is drawn 0.4 m wider and 0.6 m taller than the building, which removes the flicker while zooming.

**Controls and layout**
- **Map controls moved into the map** (zoom, compass, Home, 2D/3D), in the ArcGIS and komoot pattern. "Reset view" (header) and "Reset camera" (panel) were duplicates and are now one Home button. "Inspect center" became a keyboard shortcut: focus the map and press Enter.
- **Left panel is now "Sea level" + "Layers" only.** The slider track is painted with the band ramp, so it doubles as a key. **Play the rise** animates today → +3 ft. The study-area and legend toggles are gone: the boundary is always shown, and the legend collapses itself.
- **Insights panel** leads with one large headline number, then the stacked chart, then a breakdown of "+N ft adds …". The "About these numbers" block became a single "Data sources & limitations" link.
- **Selected-building card redesigned:** what it is → the answer (first reached at …) → a level-by-level ladder → height and footprint → the caveat. Esc or × clears it.

**Typography:** a named Roboto type scale (display 36, metric 28, title 16, heading 14, label/body 13, caption 12, overline 11) replaces ad-hoc sizes.

**Engineering**
- New pure, unit-tested modules: `src/lib/buildingPick.ts` (ray picking, tile re-joining, highlight offset) and `bandsFor` in `src/lib/manifest.ts`.
- Fixed a camera bug where an interrupted animation could flip 2D/3D back (only user gestures now sync the toggle).
- Fixed a playback bug where rapid ticks repeated a level (found by a new unit test).
- Tests: 75 unit/component tests, 12 end-to-end tests (desktop and mobile).

Inspiration (Mobbin): Felt (legend card), komoot (in-map control stack), Apple Weather and The Weather Channel (stepped colour key, playable timeline), Kiwi.com and Expedia (selected-item cards).
